Import rides

Drop in a .csv or Excel .xlsx export - like the ride exports from Saferide - and every row becomes a booking on the dashboard. The importer reads the column headers and figures out what's what (ride ID, status, vehicle, passenger, addresses, dates, and more when the file has them). Rides you've already imported are recognized by their ID and won't duplicate - if their status changed in the new file, it updates.
